# Kairon Signal Legend

Kairon signals are analytical classifications, not trade orders. They are designed
to make technical market conditions easier to read, but they do not provide
financial advice, investment recommendations, trade signals, or automated trading.

| Signal | Classification label | Signal interpretation | Research risk level | Research classification | Plain English meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WATCH_BUY | Positive Technical Structure | The asset shows constructive technical structure under this rule set and may deserve further observation. | Medium | Observation / Confirmation Required | The asset is above the moving-average thresholds used by this educational model. |
| OVERBOUGHT_WAIT | Extended Technical Structure | The asset appears extended under this rule set. | Medium-High | Extended Condition | The asset may be extended after a strong move. |
| HOLD | Neutral Technical Structure | The rule set does not identify a clear positive or defensive technical condition. | Low-Medium | Neutral Condition | There is no clear technical condition. |
| WEAKNESS_AVOID | Technical Weakness | The asset remains technically weak under this rule set. | High | Weakness Condition | The asset is trading below key moving averages and shows short-term weakness. |
| OVERSOLD_WATCH | Oversold Observation | The asset shows an oversold reading under this rule set and requires further observation. | High | Oversold Observation | The asset may be oversold, but that reading alone does not imply a favorable condition. |
| OVERSOLD_BUT_WEAK | Oversold With Weak Trend | The asset shows an oversold reading while trend measures remain weak under this rule set. | Very High | Defensive / Weak Trend | The asset looks oversold, but the trend remains weak. This may represent elevated downside risk. |
