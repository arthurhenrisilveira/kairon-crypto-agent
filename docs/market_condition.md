# Market Condition Methodology

Kairon v0.2 adds an explainable Market Condition field to the terminal output and Markdown market report.

Market Condition is an educational classification of the current group of tracked assets. It summarizes how the existing signal labels are distributed across the analyzed assets.

It does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, trade signals, automated trading, or instructions for handling any asset.

## Inputs

Market Condition uses only the signal labels already generated by `src/strategy_rules.py`:

- `WATCH_BUY`
- `OVERBOUGHT_WAIT`
- `HOLD`
- `WEAKNESS_AVOID`
- `OVERSOLD_WATCH`
- `OVERSOLD_BUT_WEAK`

The signal names remain unchanged for compatibility with existing analysis, CSV exports, Markdown reports, and backtests.

## Signal Distribution

The helper functions in `src/market_condition.py` count how many tracked assets have each signal.

For example, if four assets are analyzed and three are `HOLD` while one is `OVERBOUGHT_WAIT`, the distribution is:

- `HOLD`: 3 assets
- `OVERBOUGHT_WAIT`: 1 asset
- all other signals: 0 assets

The Market Condition explanation uses those counts to describe why a classification was assigned.

## Classification Rules

The rules are intentionally simple and transparent:

- `Mixed`: at least one asset shows positive technical structure and at least one asset shows defensive technical weakness.
- `Constructive`: most assets show `WATCH_BUY`, and no assets show defensive weakness.
- `Defensive / Weak`: two or more assets show `WEAKNESS_AVOID` or `OVERSOLD_BUT_WEAK`.
- `Extended / Cautious`: two or more assets show `OVERBOUGHT_WAIT`.
- `Neutral`: most assets show `HOLD`, or no stronger rule applies.
- `Unavailable`: no asset results are available.

These classifications describe the rule set's view of the tracked asset group. They are not market advice.

## Main Drivers

The report also lists main drivers, grouped by signal.

Examples:

- BTCUSDT, BNBUSDT, and SOLUSDT are classified as neutral by the current rule set.
- ETHUSDT appears extended under the RSI rule.
- BTCUSDT remains technically weak under the moving-average rule.

Drivers are included to make the classification easier to audit.

## Limitations

Market Condition is based only on simple technical rules, a small asset list, and the latest available public market data used by the local run.

It does not include:

- exchange account data
- API keys
- trade execution
- portfolio context
- risk tolerance
- liquidity depth
- macroeconomic events
- on-chain data
- sentiment data

The classification can change quickly when public market data changes. Historical behavior and backtest results do not guarantee future behavior.

## Educational Disclaimer

Kairon Crypto Agent is for educational and research purposes only. Market Condition is an analytical classification generated by simple technical rules. It is not financial advice, an investment recommendation, a trade signal, or automated trading output.
